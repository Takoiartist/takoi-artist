ABOUT

▶

With years of experience in design and a growing community across digital platforms, Jotako Studio blends art, inclusion, and technology to offer:

  • Custom digital illustration services
  • Original comic production
  • 2D animation with a strong narrative focus
  • Online classes and resources for emerging artists

Beyond creating, Jotako teaches. Through online courses, he shares his love for artistic anatomy, expressive drawing, and character design—always from an inclusive and diversity-conscious approach.

This space is more than a portfolio—it's a window into the creative process, a hub for learning, and a bridge for meaningful collaboration.
Welcome to Jotako Studio, where every line tells a story.

Takoii Artist
Jorge Armando Castaño

I’m Jorge A. Castaño, an illustrator, comic artist, and 2D animator. I’m passionate about telling stories that connect emotionally, using drawing and animation as languages to express ideas, perspectives, and feelings in an authentic way with strong visual impact.

My main inspiration comes from Japanese visual narrative, especially manga and anime. From the beginning, I focused on exploring the manga style, studying the human figure and creating charismatic characters—those that feel alive the moment you see them. This interest shaped my path and, over time, led me to explore different styles, themes, and techniques with curiosity and openness to change.

I mainly work with Clip Studio Paint and Adobe Illustrator, and I enjoy experimenting with digital illustration and animation tools such as MediBang, Procreate, and OpenToonz. I like to analyze techniques, adapt to different visual styles, and find the right approach for each project.

Beyond work, my hobbies are an essential part of who I am: reading manga, watching anime, enjoying classic cinema, and drawing both on paper and digitally. All of this keeps me connected to creation and gives meaning to what I do. It is, quite literally, my raison d’être.

Under the pseudonym Tako Iwakura, abbreviated as Takoi, my goal is to continue developing 2D animation for different formats and themes, as well as creating illustrations that tell engaging and relatable stories. As a personal project, I am currently writing and drawing the webcomic DREAMERS ~Soñadores~, published on Webtoon Canvas, Tapas, and Akaya.
