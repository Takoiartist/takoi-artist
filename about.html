ABOUT US

▶

At Takoi Artist, we aim to develop and produce bold animated projects that redefine visual entertainment. Under the creative direction of visual designers Jorge Armando Castaño and Melisa Sánchez, we seamlessly blend conceptual art with commercial viability, the magic of animation with licensing and merchandising strategy, and global trends with our own local identity.

  • Custom digital illustration services
  • Original comic production
  • 2D animation with a strong narrative focus
  • Online classes and resources for emerging artists

This space is more than a portfolio—it's a window into our creative process, a hub for learning, and a bridge for meaningful collaboration.
Welcome to Takoi Artist, where every line tells a story.

MEET THE TEAM

Takoi Artist Jorge Armando Castaño

I'm Jorge A. Castaño, an illustrator, comic artist, and 2D animator. I'm passionate about telling stories that connect emotionally, using drawing and animation as languages to express ideas, perspectives, and feelings in an authentic way with strong visual impact.

My main inspiration comes from Japanese visual narrative, especially manga and anime. From the beginning, I focused on exploring the manga style, studying the human figure and creating charismatic characters—those that feel alive the moment you see them. This interest shaped my path and, over time, led me to explore different styles, themes, and techniques with curiosity and openness to change.
